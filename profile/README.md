# Welcome to Sanctania 👋

**Sanctania** is an organization dedicated to building a classic-style MMORPG that pushes the genre to its limits without compromising on ethics or craft.

## ⚖️ The Nine Commandments (Our Ethics)

Sanctania is sacred and its authenticity must be preserved at all cost. We strictly adhere to these core principles:

1. No cashshop, No P2W (Pay to Win), No cosmetics, No participation trophy.
2. Subscription + Donation only business model.
3. Contributors will be compensated fairly.
4. No internal & external RMT (Real Money Trading).
5. Immediate and permanent bans for cheaters.
6. No auto bans from mass reporting.
7. Real game masters voted by the community every season.
8. No catering to e-sports.
9. No politics, no agendas.
